## Highlights

- MiniCPM-V-4.7-35B-A3B (EXL3 6.08 bits per weight, SAGE) on one GB10: 262,144-token window, 4 slots, speculative decoding with a DSpark draft pack, prefix cache and image input on (`minicpmv47-single-262k`, port 8889). SAGE is our own mixed-precision quantization method for EXL3. The method is not published. Measured in its qualification on one GB10 (engine 0.1.1): 112.2 tok/s generation for one stream (median of 3 runs of 5 replies of 256 tokens, draft on), prompt speed 2,706 tok/s at 131,097 tokens and 1,884 tok/s at 260,189 tokens (cold prompts, median of 3 runs); Q200 183 of 200 (91.5%) with thinking on, greedy text suites with an 8,192-token budget. Thinking is off unless a request sets `enable_thinking`. Details and methods in `docs/packs/minicpm-v-4.7-35b-a3b.md`.
